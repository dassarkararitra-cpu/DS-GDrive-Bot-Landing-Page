Privacy Policy

Welcome to the Privacy Policy for DS GDrive Bot. Your privacy and security are our highest priorities. This policy outlines how we handle your data when you use our Telegram bot to interact with Google Drive.

1. Data Collection & Google OAuth Tokens

To provide file streaming services, DS GDrive Bot requires authorization to access your Google Drive via official Google OAuth 2.0. We explicitly state that all user Google OAuth tokens are securely encrypted at rest.

2. Strict Usage of Data (Google Limited Use)

DS GDrive Bot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Your OAuth tokens and access permissions are used strictly for providing or improving the app's functionality (i.e., fulfilling your authorized Google Drive file transfers). The bot does not autonomously read, modify, or delete files without your direct command.

3. No Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information, Telegram ID, or Google Drive data with any third parties. Your encrypted tokens are used exclusively by our backend to fulfill your explicit requests.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not retain or store your actual Google Drive files on our servers. We only store your encrypted OAuth tokens and Telegram ID to facilitate your requested file transfers. You can revoke this OAuth access at any time directly from your Google Account settings. To request the complete and immediate deletion of all your stored data from our database, please contact the developer on Telegram at @aritrads.
