App Functionality & Features

DS GDrive Bot is a Telegram automation tool designed to bridge your Telegram chats directly with your personal Google Drive account. Here is exactly what the bot does:

1. Direct File Uploads & Link Streaming

After connecting your Drive via the /start command, you can send any file, video, or audio directly to the bot. You can also send direct HTTP/HTTPS links. The bot will automatically stream and upload these files directly into your Google Drive storage without requiring you to download them to your device first.

2. Instant GDrive Cloning

If you send a public Google Drive link to the bot, it utilizes the Google Drive API to instantly clone the file directly into your own Google Drive. This bypasses the server download phase entirely for lightning-fast copies.

3. Drive Explorer & File Management

Using the /manage command, you can interact with your Google Drive directly from Telegram. The bot allows you to create new folders, rename files, delete items, and set specific folder paths for your future uploads.

4. Storage & Transfer Analytics

The bot provides full visibility into your operations. Use /stats to view your upload history, average speeds, and transfer success rates. Use /driveinfo to check your Google Drive's total capacity, used space, and free space. You can disconnect your account at any time using /logout.

Welcome to the Privacy Policy for DS GDrive Bot, developed by Aritra Das Sarkar (@aritrads). Your privacy and security are our highest priorities. This policy outlines how we collect, use, protect, and handle your data when you use our Telegram bot to interact with Google Drive.

1. Information We Collect

When you use DS GDrive Bot, our application will access the data you have provided to us via official Google OAuth 2.0. We specifically collect and securely store your Google OAuth access and refresh tokens, as well as your basic Telegram User ID to link your chat session. We request access to view, edit, create, and delete only the specific Google Drive files you interact with through the bot.

2. How We Use Your Information

We will use your data solely to provide you with the services you requested. Specifically, your OAuth tokens are used to stream files from Telegram directly into your Google Drive, clone Google Drive links, and allow you to manage (rename, delete, move) your files directly from the Telegram interface. We do not use your data for advertising, market research, or to train artificial intelligence models.

3. Data Sharing and Transfer

We do not sell your data to third parties. We do not transfer or disclose your information to third parties for purposes other than the ones provided (providing the core file-transfer service), unless explicitly required by applicable law.

4. Data Security

Security procedures are in place to protect the confidentiality of your data. We use encryption to protect your information, specifically securing your OAuth tokens both in transit and at rest. No human developer or third party is permitted to access, read, or download your Google Drive files.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your encrypted Google OAuth tokens only for as long as you maintain an active connection with the bot. We do not store your actual files on our servers. You may revoke the bot's access at any time via your Google Account security settings.
